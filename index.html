About us

A small, focused IT consultancy.

AX CONSULTING LTD is an information technology consultancy based in Fetcham, Leatherhead, in Surrey. The company was incorporated on 15 April 2015 and is registered in England and Wales under company number 09544659.

We work with businesses and organisations that need IT expertise: planning technology decisions, designing and reviewing systems, and supporting IT change through to delivery.

As a director-led limited company, we keep things direct. There are no agency layers between you and the person doing the work, just clear advice and hands-on delivery.

Services

How we can help

Four areas of IT consultancy, from early planning to independent review of what is already in place.

IT Strategy & Advisory

Helping organisations plan technology decisions and match their systems to business goals, so investment goes where it makes the most difference.

  • Technology planning and priorities
  • Options appraisal for new or replacement systems
  • Advice that links IT choices to business goals

Systems & Infrastructure

Consultancy on designing, reviewing and improving IT systems and infrastructure, whether you are planning something new or getting more from what you have.

  • Planning and design of systems and infrastructure
  • Review of existing environments
  • Practical recommendations for improvement

Project Delivery

Hands-on support to plan and deliver IT change projects from start to finish, keeping scope, people and progress pointed in the same direction.

  • Scoping and planning of IT change
  • Coordination through each stage of delivery
  • Hands-on support until the change is in place

Technical Review

Independent assessment of existing setups to find risks and fixes, with findings explained in plain English rather than jargon.

  • Independent assessment of current setups
  • Identifying risks and practical fixes
  • Troubleshooting of technical issues
